Undergraduate
Thesis

09 / 2024 – 07 / 2025

Sustainable Thermal Energy Storage Solutions Using Coated Perlite-Encapsulated PCMs with Nanostructured Enhancements

Supervisor: Dr. Md. Abu Mowazzem Hossain — Professor, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, CUET

Investigated sustainable thermal energy storage by developing coated perlite-encapsulated phase change materials (PCMs) with nanostructured enhancements to address low thermal conductivity limitations. Conducted systematic thermal conductivity experiments and comprehensive material characterization to analyze morphology, crystallinity, and phase transition behavior. Managed end-to-end experimental design, laboratory planning, data collection, and technical reporting. Results demonstrated improved thermal conductivity, enhanced phase stability, and reliable structural integrity — indicating strong potential for efficient and scalable thermal energy storage applications.
